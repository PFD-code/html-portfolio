The objectively best films by Pierre-François

My top 3 movies of all-times

Cyrano de Bergerac

by Jean-Paul Rappeneau
A classic of the French cinema. A true masterpiece of realisation and scenario that tells a lot in a short time. Leave the usual clichés of white knight in shining armor at the door, and hop into a story where a man can not hold every qualities at once, and will have to make sacrifices.
A reflection on the role of appearance in society, as we wonder : can a man judged ugly still thrive at the forefront of the scene ? Or will he have to burn himself to shine ?

Tenue de soirée

by Bertrand Blier
If you know who Bertrand Blier is, you know what kind of film to expect here. If you don't, tighten your seatbelts and prepare for a full throttle start right from the get go.
Discover or rediscover 3 of the main French movie stars of the 80s in a fast paced comedy where we follow a life segment of a couple struggling financialy as they meet a seemingly miraculous man. The film lasts a bit longer than an hour and twenty minutes, but feels much shorter. At the end of the movie, you will be left with a mixed bag of emotions, many questions, and you probably will be wondering what the point of the film was.
All that is perfectly normal, as we said, it's Bertrand Blier.

Hadjime No Ippo

by George Morikawa
A not so hidden gem of an anime depicting the life of a young man getting into boxing. The author makes clever use of the Shonen genre, with efficient and to the point storytelling, without falling into the usual traps of the main character winning everytime and shadowing the rest of the cast.
Viewers new to boxing will discover the fascinating world of this sport, blending raw physicality, tactical genius, carrier management and team effort, forming a discipline in which every individual can fully express their range of qualities, far away from the clichés of Rocky style movies. At the same time, people already experienced in boxing will appreciate the numerous references made by the author to legendary All of Famers of the sport, as well as very precise drawing of the body positions, and real tactics and techniques used as of today by peak performing boxers. A masterpiece of the boxing genre.
